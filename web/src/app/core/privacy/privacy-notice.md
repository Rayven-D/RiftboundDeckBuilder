# Privacy notice

Effective 2026-11-25 · Last updated 2026-11-25

## Who we are

Rift Builder, an independent fan project, is responsible for your data. It is free and non-commercial, and based in the United States. Contact: [riftbuilder.support@gmail.com](mailto:riftbuilder.support@gmail.com). Rift Builder is not affiliated with Riot Games. *Rift Builder was created under Riot Games' "Legal Jibber Jabber" policy using assets owned by Riot Games. Riot Games does not endorse or sponsor this project.*

## What we store

You can use Home and Tournaments without an account; Rift Builder stores nothing in the cloud for that. To import cards or decks you create an account with your email address. We store:

- your email address and a user ID;
- your username, the display name you choose (it is shown only to you, at the top of the page; you can leave it out);
- your collection: card identifiers, counts and printing identifiers;
- your tags: labels, colours and settings;
- your decks: names, where each came from, notes and card lists;
- the times you locked decks, timestamps of changes, and the version of the app that last wrote your data.

Your username, tag labels, deck names and notes are free text that you control. Please do not put personal information in them.

## What Google collects for us

Our hosting and database provider is Google (Firebase). When you sign in, Google also keeps the IP address and user agent of the sign-in requests, for a few weeks, to keep accounts secure and stop abuse. Sign-in emails are sent from noreply@riftbound-deckbuilder.firebaseapp.com; if you reply to one, the reply goes to riftbuilder.support@gmail.com.

## What we do not collect

- No analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
- No Riot account, Riot login or Riot account data. Rift Builder does not use Riot Sign On.
- No payment information.
- No account data about you when you only visit Home or Tournaments. Google, which hosts our pages, may keep standard request logs, which can include your IP address, for any visit, as any web host does.

## Why we use it

To run your account, keep your collection, tags, decks and locks in sync across your devices, keep the service secure, and delete your data when you ask. We do not use it for marketing and we do not sell or share it.

## Legal basis (for people in the EU and UK)

We use your data to give you the service you ask for, and, for security logs, because keeping the service safe is in our legitimate interest. We do not rely on consent.

## Who receives it

Google (Firebase), our hosting and database provider, handles the data for us, including the request logs above. No one else receives it, and no one buys it.

## Where it is stored

Your data is stored by Google Firebase in the United States. If you are outside the US, your data is transferred to the US; Google protects such transfers with the mechanisms it offers (the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses).

## Who can read it

Only you, signed in, can read your data through the app, and we do not look at anyone's collection or decks. The project owner can technically open the database as an administrator; we only do so to fix a problem or when you ask.

## What your browser keeps

While you are signed in, your browser keeps a copy of your account's data (so pages load quickly and work offline), your view settings, the email address you typed while signing in by email link (removed when sign-in finishes), and your sign-in session, all in the browser's own storage on your device. Your browser also remembers your light or dark theme on that device; it is not part of your account, so signing out or deleting your account does not clear it. Rift Builder sets no cookies. We keep only what the features you asked for need, so there is no cookie banner. Signing out or deleting your account clears the copy of your account's data from the device.

## Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

We do not track you across websites or over time, and we do not sell or share your data, so there is nothing to opt out of. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals therefore change nothing. No other party tracks you on this site through us.

## How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. When you sign out, the copy on your device is cleared. After you delete your account, Google removes the data from its systems within about 180 days. Sign-in IP logs are kept for a few weeks.

## Your choices

- Change what you store in the app at any time.
- Delete everything: open the account menu, choose **Account Information**, then **Delete account and data**. This deletes your account and the collection, tags, decks and locks saved in it, on every device.
- For anything else, write to [riftbuilder.support@gmail.com](mailto:riftbuilder.support@gmail.com).

## Your rights (people in the EU and UK)

You can ask for access to your data, correction, erasure, restriction, to object to our use of it, and a portable copy. Write to [riftbuilder.support@gmail.com](mailto:riftbuilder.support@gmail.com); we aim to answer within one month. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

## Is giving us data required?

An email address is needed only to make an account. Without an account you can still use Home and Tournaments.

## No automated decisions

We do not make automated decisions or profile you.

## Children

Rift Builder is not for anyone under 16. We do not check ages, and we do not knowingly collect data from people under 16. If we learn that someone under 16 has an account, we delete the account and its data.

## Security

Data travels over HTTPS and is encrypted at rest by Google. If a breach affects you and the law requires it, we will tell you.

## Changes

When this notice changes, we update the date at the top and describe the change in our public changelog ([the What's new page](/changelog)).
