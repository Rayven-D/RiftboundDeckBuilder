# UI Review 2026-11-24: sign-in gate, account menu and dialogs

## Summary
> **What:** a design review of `loop/SPEC-010-v0.9-build` (`2ff31e5`): dev's H-085 fluid pass merged with the
> SPEC-010 v0.9 sign-in UI. I looked at it live on `http://localhost:4201` (`signInEnabled: true`, no emulator)
> at 1440, 1024 and 375 px, in dark and light, with the keyboard. The signed-in states (account menu, deletion
> error, sign-out warning, delete dialog) were rendered by setting `SyncService` signals from the console,
> not by a real sign-in. Code read: `app.html`, `core/sync/*`, `shared/sync/status-text.ts`, `shared/empty-state.ts`, `styles.scss`.
>
> **Verdict:** the gate is functionally right and matches the SYNC-009 strings. Three problems should be fixed
> before RJ tries it on a phone: **(1)** keyboard focus is invisible on every Material button (Import, Sign in,
> dialog buttons), **(2)** snack bars cover the phone tab bar, and **(3)** "Delete account" is styled as the teal
> primary action. After those, most of the gains are in the email-link flow (one step too many, weak error
> state, no resend, native `prompt()`) and in the account menu (no grouping, no "signed in as", no
> danger styling).
>
> **Owners:** 23 findings. 15 have builder work and 11 need spec-writer (they change SPEC-010 text or
> behavior; 4, 5, 8 and 19 are split between the two). Item 23 is RJ's choice.
> **Filed 2026-11-24:** H-101 to H-126 and H-139 on the [[Handoff Board]], one row per finding, with spec-writer and builder rows for #4, #5, #8 and #19.
> Screenshots: `.playwright-mcp/ui-review/` in the worktree (not committed).

## Findings, ranked by impact

| # | Screen / state | What's wrong | Recommendation | Owner | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | All pages: header **Import** and **Sign in / Account**, gate **Sign in**, every dialog button | **No visible keyboard focus.** The global `:focus-visible { outline: 2px solid var(--color-focus) }` is overridden by Material's `outline: none`; the only cue is Material's faint state-layer tint (e.g. teal filled Import shows nothing at all). Measured: focused `Import` has `outline: none` and no ring (`decks-focus-import-1440-dark.png`, `decks-focus-signin-1440-dark.png`). Fails WCAG 2.4.7 in practice. Likely came with H-085, but the sign-in UI is all Material buttons, so it hits every new control. | Give `.mat-mdc-button-base:focus-visible` (and `mat-menu-item`, form fields' buttons) the same 2px `--color-focus` outline with 2px offset, or include Material's `strong-focus-indicators` themed to `--color-focus`. Never animate it. **Accept:** Tab through header, gate panel, sign-in dialog and account menu at 1440 and 375, dark and light: every stop shows a ring ≥ 3:1 against its background. | builder | S |
| 2 | Phone (< 40rem), any snack bar (sync notices, "Some data couldn't be deleted.", the persistent "You've been signed out…") | **Snack bars sit on top of the bottom tab bar** and hide Decks / Collection / Tournaments / Import for 8 s, or until OK for the session-lost notice (`snackbar-375-light.png`). | Offset the snack-bar container on phones by `var(--tabbar-height) + env(safe-area-inset-bottom) + var(--space-2)` (global style or a `panelClass` set in `SyncService.notify` and `AccountMenu`). **Accept:** 375 px screenshot with a notice open shows all four tabs fully visible and tappable. | builder | S |
| 3 | Delete account dialog; account menu item **Delete account and data** | **The destructive action looks like the primary action.** The template sets `class="danger"` on Delete account but no `.danger` style exists, so it renders as the teal filled button (`delete-dialog-1440-light.png`). In the menu, Delete account and data sits directly under Sign out with no separation. | Add a danger button variant from the existing `--color-danger` token (dark `#f09595`, light `#a32d2d`): filled danger with a contrast-checked label, or text/outlined danger (pick one, add it to `/styleguide`). In the menu, put a `mat-divider` before Delete account and data and color its label `--color-danger`. **Accept:** delete button label contrast ≥ 4.5:1 in dark and light; the button is not teal; `/styleguide` shows the variant. | builder | S |
| 4 | Sign-in dialog, email-link flow (only provider at launch) | **One step too many, and two primaries.** Click "Sign in with an email link" → the field appears below, the provider button stays filled, and "Send link" is a second filled button (`signin-email-1440-dark.png`). Focus stays on the provider button, so a keyboard user must Tab to the field (checked). | **Builder now:** after the reveal, move focus to the email field, and turn the provider button into a non-primary state (outlined, or hide it when it is the only provider). **Spec change:** when `authProviders` has only `email-link`, show the email field and **Send link** straight away under the intro (no provider button). | builder (focus, button state); spec-writer (SYNC-006 single-provider layout) | S |
| 5 | Sign-in dialog, Send link failure | Error is plain muted text with no icon, the field isn't marked invalid, and the text is the SYNC-005 sign-in failure: "Sign-in didn't complete. Your data on this device is unchanged." In account-required mode a signed-out device **has** no data, and the user didn't sign in yet, they asked for a link (`signin-error-1440-dark.png`). No "Sending…" state while waiting. | **Builder:** error styling (icon + `--color-danger` text, `aria-invalid` + `aria-describedby` on the field), and "Sending…" on the button while `sending()`. **Spec:** a separate send-failure string, e.g. "Couldn't send the link. Check the address and your connection, then try again." | builder (styling, sending state); spec-writer (string) | S |
| 6 | Sign-in dialog, after Send link | "Check your email for a sign-in link." is a dead end: no address shown, no resend, no "use a different address", no hint that the tab can stay open. Users of email links often wait, check spam, or mistype. | Spec: "We sent a sign-in link to **{address}**. Open it on this device to finish. Didn't get it? **Send again** · **Use a different email**". Builder then shows it as a status block with the two text buttons. | spec-writer | S |
| 7 | Email link opened on another device | Uses the browser's native `prompt()` ("Confirm your email address to finish signing in."): unstyled, outside the app's look, can be suppressed in some in-app browsers. UI Index lists it as open. | Replace with a small Material dialog in the sign-in dialog's style (same text, an email field, **Finish sign-in** / Cancel; Cancel behaves like an empty prompt, SYNC-005). Text stays as specced. | builder | M |
| 8 | Account menu (signed in) | **No grouping and no identity.** Items run status → Try again → Cancel deletion → Sync now → Sign out → Delete in one undivided list (`account-menu-deletion-1440-light.png`), and nothing says which account is signed in, which matters with email links and shared devices. | **Builder:** dividers between groups: [status] / [Try again, Cancel deletion] / [Sync now] / [Sign out] / [Delete account and data, danger]. **Spec:** a first line "Signed in as {email}" (shown from Auth, never synced; check against SYNC-100 privacy). | builder (dividers); spec-writer (identity line) | S |
| 9 | Account menu during an unfinished deletion | **Sync now is offered while sync is blocked.** SYNC-096 says the device does no pull and no push while `deletingUid` is set, yet Sync now is enabled and the status adds "· 3 changes not synced yet" next to the deletion error. Two "try" actions compete (Try again vs Sync now). | Spec decision: hide or disable Sync now while `deletingUid` matches, and drop the pending count from the deletion error line. Consider labelling the menu action "Try deleting again" (the notice can keep "Try again"). | spec-writer | S |
| 10 | Delete account dialog with email-link sign-in older than 5 min | The user reads the warning, types `delete`, presses Delete account, and only then is told to sign out, sign in again and come back within 5 minutes (SYNC-096 step 1). | 11 | Dialogs in dark mode (Sign in, Sign out, Delete) | **The dialog surface is the page background** (`rgb(27,31,36)` = `--color-bg`) because `--mat-sys-surface` maps to `--color-bg`. ## What works
- The gate matches SYNC-009: headings per page, the exact signed-out text, page header kept, header tabs and Import usable, deep link `?type=` kept until the gate opens.
- The sign-in dialog opens with focus on its first button and returns focus to the trigger on a normal close; Escape closes it. Sign-out opens with focus on **Cancel** (safe default).
- Status is text (SYNC-084), menu items are 48 px tall, gate and header buttons have a 48 px touch area, the phone tab bar is 64 px.
- No console errors other than the expected emulator connection refusal on Send link.

## Not checked
- A real sign-in, real first sync, real offline toggling (no emulator); the 768 px width (1024 and 375 covered); reduced motion on the new dialogs (the global reduced-motion block should cover Material's animations, worth a builder check with item 13's progress bar).
