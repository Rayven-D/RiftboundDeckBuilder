# Design Direction

Owner: **ui-builder** with RJ. Status: **decided 2026-11-23: direction A (Table Tool), blue accent `#378ADD`** (changed from teal the same day), with the changes listed under "Decision" below.

Hard limits for every direction ([[Riot Developer Rules]], [[Product Vision]]): our own visual
identity, **no Riot or Riftbound logos**, nothing designed to look like Riot's official site or
the game's UI, and no "official" wording. Card art from the API is allowed and is the visual
centrepiece. The Legal Jibber Jabber notice always sits in the footer.

## Decision (RJ, 2026-11-23)
- **Direction A, Table Tool**, with a **blue accent `#378ADD`** (RJ changed it from teal `#1D9E75` on 2026-11-23). Light mode uses `#185FA5`. Labels on the blue fill are dark navy `#031A33` (4.9:1). Accent as *text* on slate uses the lighter `#85B7EB` (`--color-accent-text`), because `#378ADD` is only 4.0:1 on the surface. Dark slate surfaces: bg `#1b1f24`, surface `#242a31`, raised `#2d343c`, border `#343c45`.
- **Ownership label borrowed from B/C:** a chip ("1 of 3 owned") with a state icon. Fully owned is filled with the blue accent; partly owned has an accent outline; none owned is a plain outlined chip. Always text plus icon (SPEC-005 OWN-033).
- **Deck source line is secondary:** the "1st, Regional Championship: Europe · dates" line under a deck name is small (`--font-size-xs`) and muted, read as a description rather than a heading.
- **Import is the main action:** it moved out of the tabs to the far right of the header as a raised, accent-filled button with an upload icon. Tabs are now Your decks, My collection, Tournaments.
- **Angular Material (M3) is allowed** (RJ: "don't be afraid to use it"). It is themed from the accent (`web/src/_theme-colors.scss`, generated by `ng generate @angular/material:theme-color`) with its corner tokens set to the same 4 / 6 / 8 px radii, and its surfaces mapped to the slate tokens.
- **Icons:** inline SVG from Material Symbols (Apache 2.0) through `app-icon` (`web/src/app/shared/icon.ts`); only the icons in use are shipped, instead of a 4 MB icon font and with no Google Fonts request.
- **Built (branch `loop/ui-direction-a`):** theme and tokens, header, `app-ownership-chip`, `app-card-tile` (full / partial left-to-right / none treatment, 1 px divider at the fill edge), `app-deck-heading`, and a preview page at `/styleguide` (sample data, not in the nav).

## Screen names (RJ, 2026-11-23)
- Tabs and page titles are **Decks** and **Collection** (were "Your decks" and "My collection"). "Collection" over "Cards": "Cards" reads as a database of every card (a likely later feature), while Collection is what you own and matches the Import card's title.
- In running text and buttons, keep plain sentence wording: "Add to your decks", "In your decks", "one click into your decks".

## Home page and headline font (RJ, 2026-11-23)
- Reference: riftatlas.com (layout ideas only; our own identity). Ideas came from ui-builder; RJ picked:
  - **`/` is the home page for everyone** (was a redirect to `/decks`); the wordmark links home; unknown paths go home.
  - **Headline:** "Know what you own. / Own what you need." (second line in `--color-accent-text`).
  - **Hero visual: ownership fan** (`pages/home/ownership-fan.ts`): three tilted placeholder cards (missing, 1 of 3, 3 of 3) with their chips, our deck-stack mark faded behind. Real card art drops into the same `app-card-art` later.
  - **Headline font: Archivo**, self-hosted (`@fontsource-variable/archivo`, OFL, no Google Fonts request), heavy (800) and semi-expanded (112%), via `--font-display`. Used for h1, h2, `.display` and the wordmark; body text stays the system font.
- Page sections: hero (two import buttons, "Free · No account · Stored in your browser", arrow links); three step tiles with dividers (Add your cards, Add a deck, See what's missing); "Ownership" feature with a framed example deck panel; "Tournaments" feature with an example Top 8 panel (placings only, no stats). Example content is labelled "example".
- Shared pieces added: `.eyebrow` (mono uppercase label), `.arrow-link` (text link with →), `app-card-art` (ownership image treatment, used by the tile and the fan), `sampleCardArt()` (abstract placeholder art).
- Not copied on purpose: Rift Atlas's font, cyan, wording; Riot's domain symbols; Play / Sealed; a card search box (no card data yet); language picker; anything popularity-based.
- Later ideas from ui-builder, not built yet: returning-user summary in the hero ("3 decks · 412 cards · Continue"), card search with a Ctrl+K hint once card data exists, footer Privacy / About / Contact, a dismissible "Card data coming soon" banner, `kbd` hints.

## Corners (RJ, 2026-11-23, later the same day)
- Square corners felt too sharp. Now a slight radius: **4px** small controls and chips (`--radius-sm`), **6px** buttons and card art (`--radius-md`), **8px** cards, panels and dialogs (`--radius-lg`). Material corner tokens match (buttons 6px, dialogs 8px, inputs 4px).
- Nav tabs round the **top corners only**, so the active tab's 2px underline stays straight (earlier feedback).
- Import cards keep their left (accent) side square so the blue edge is a straight bar; only the right corners are rounded.

## Import screen (RJ, 2026-11-23)
- Two clickable cards (whole card is a button, with icon, accent edge and chevron): **Collection**, "Import cards to your collection", and **Deck**, "Import deck lists to your decks". Each opens a dialog (**Import collection** / **Import deck**).
- The dialog has a text box to paste into and an **Upload file** button for CSV, Excel (.xlsx) or text. An upload is written into the text box (one line per row) so it can be checked and edited the same way. Every line needs a number then the name: `1 Name` or `1x Name`; CSV / Excel rows follow the same rule. Live summary: cards and lines found, lines that need fixing (with line numbers), and section totals for decks. Bad lines are left out; Continue needs at least one good line.
- Until card data and storage exist, Continue shows the parsed list on the Import page marked "Not saved yet". Empty states link to `/import?type=collection` or `?type=deck`, which opens that dialog.
- Excel support (`read-excel-file`, MIT) is loaded only when an .xlsx is picked.

## Footer notice (RJ, 2026-11-23)
- Riot's Legal Jibber Jabber statement keeps Riot's wording (it is the required notice, [[Card Data & Images]] #6) but is set at the extra-small size with tight padding, so it takes one or two lines.

## Candidate directions (kept for history)

### A. Table Tool (calm utility)
Quiet dark-slate surfaces, one cool accent, system fonts, and dense, readable lists. The card images
supply all the colour, and the chrome stays out of the way. It's optimised for a phone at the game
table: big tap targets, sticky filters, and owned/missing shown with icon + colour + text. It's the
lowest effort, the most clearly "not Riot", and it ages well.

### B. Binder (collector's feel)
Warm neutral paper and leather tones, with cards in sleeve-like tiles on a binder-page grid. A
completion % shows as a thin progress bar on each deck tile, and a serif is used for headings only.
It feels personal and collection-first. There's a moderate risk of looking busy, so keep the
ornament minimal.

### C. Scoreboard (event-forward)
High-contrast dark theme with bold sans headings and placing labels ("1st", "Top 4") as strong
badges, plus an event timeline on Tournaments. It celebrates the players and results (Riot rule 8:
honour, don't shame). We must take care that it doesn't drift toward the game's own
fantasy-UI styling.

## Decisions taken in the scaffold (H-009, 2026-11-23)
- **Stack:** Angular 22.3 standalone components and signals, zoneless (the v22 default), lazy-loaded
  routes, SCSS, strict TypeScript, no SSR ([[ADR-0001 Tech Stack]]).
- **Routes:** `/decks` (default), `/decks/:id`, `/collection`, `/import`, `/tournaments`. Unknown
  paths redirect to `/decks`. Route params bind to component inputs.
- **Shell:** a plain-text wordmark "Rift Builder" (renamed 2026-11-23; was "Riftbound Deck Builder", then briefly "Rift Decks") (no logo, no imitation of official
  lettering), a nav list, a skip link, `<main>`, and a footer with the Legal Jibber Jabber notice.
- **Tokens:** CSS custom properties in `web/src/styles.scss`. The neutral palette is dark by default
  and follows `prefers-color-scheme` for light. There's a 4px spacing scale, radii, system font
  stack, and `--owned` / `--missing` colours (never colour alone in the UI: always pair with
  text or an icon).
- **Card images:** a `--card-aspect-ratio: 63 / 88` placeholder (standard TCG card size, *to
  confirm* against the images the API serves), plus a `.card-image` class that reserves space to
  avoid layout shift.
- **Config:** `apiBaseUrl` in `web/src/environments/` (dev `http://localhost:3000`, prod `/api`),
  exposed via the `API_BASE_URL` injection token. Front-end config is public, so it never holds
  secrets or keys.
- **Quality:** ESLint (angular-eslint, including template accessibility rules) and Vitest with jsdom
  (no browser needed). Scripts: `npm run lint`, `npm run build`, `npm test`.
- **Copy:** no metagame language (win rates, tiers, "meta" scores) in the UI ([[ADR-0003 Tournament Results Only]]).

## Name and favicon (RJ, 2026-11-23)
- **Product name: Rift Builder** (RJ, 2026-11-23; briefly "Rift Decks", dropped the same day because a Riftbound site called riftdecks already exists). Replaces "Riftbound Deck Builder" in the wordmark, page titles and the Legal Jibber Jabber notice. This also follows the Riot rule that trademarks can't be used in project names or domains ([[Card Data & Images]] #8); "Riftbound" is a Riot trademark. The repo and folder keep their names.
- **Favicon:** an original deck mark: three stacked square-cornered cards, blue front card (`#378ADD`) with two slate cards behind, dark outline so it reads on light and dark tabs. Files in `web/public/`: `favicon.svg` (source), `favicon.ico` (16/32/48), `apple-touch-icon.png` (180, slate background). No Riot marks.
