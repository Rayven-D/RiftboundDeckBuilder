# Meta Overview

## Summary
**The meta is resetting today.** The pre-ban Radiance meta (late Oct to mid Nov 2026) was **Riven**
(S-tier, about 41–49% of RC Europe), then **Kennen** and **Kai'Sa**. The 2026-11-23 bans hit the core of
all three: Riven's Chosen Champion *Riven, Broken Blade*, plus Zhonya's Hourglass and Stellacorn Herder;
Kennen's Nocturne and Rhasa; Kai'Sa's Brynhir Thundersong; and Star Spring and Emperor's Dais. **No
post-ban data exists yet.** Riot's own event recaps (Legend play-share tables and full lists) are the most
reliable meta signal. Community tier lists are refreshed about every 1–2 weeks.

## Conflicts / uncertainties
- No post-ban data yet. Revisit after the first weekend (2026-11-28 to 29) and **RC North America (Las Vegas, 2026-12-11 to 13)**.
- Tier lists are opinion with some data. Many win rates are given without a named source.
- **Policy risk:** Riot's developer policy forbids apps that publish or keep "metagame-defining data" (play and win rates). See [[Card Data & Images]]. Showing tiers or win rates in our app may not be allowed.
