# Ban List

## Summary
Riftbound has **bans only, no restricted or limited list** (Riot said in March 2026 it had no plans to
restrict). There are separate lists for **Standard Constructed** and **2v2 Constructed**, kept in the
"Constructed Format Legality" sections of the Rules Hub. **The newest wave took effect on 2026-11-23**,
bringing Standard to **15 cards + 7 battlefields**. 2v2 has **14 cards, 7 battlefields and 1
Legend** banned (rechecked 2026-11-24; it was 6 / 5 / 1 before the Radiance wave). Riot announced on 2026-11-13 that from 23 Nov, regions behind on set releases (Korea,
Traditional Chinese) get **separate regional ban lists**.

## Current list: 2v2 Constructed (read from the Rules Hub, "last updated November 23, 2026")
Read directly from https://playriftbound.com/en-us/rules-hub/ (official) on 2026-11-26. Names are as the hub prints them. Counts: **14 cards / 7 battlefields / 1 Legend**, which matches the hub's stated counts (fact 8).

**Legend (1):** Master Yi, Wuju Bladesman

**Cards (14):** Called Shot; Ekko, Recurrent; Draven Vanquisher (the hub's 2v2 section prints no comma; its Standard section prints "Draven, Vanquisher"; same card, normalise the name); Scrapheap; Stealthy Pursuer; Stacked Deck; Riven, Broken Blade; Zhonya's Hourglass; Stellacorn Herder; Nocturne, Horrifying; Rhasa the Sunderer; Brynhir Thundersong; Dazzling Aurora; Baited Hook.

**Battlefields (7):** The Arena's Greatest; Aspirant's Climb; Dreaming Tree (see fact 10); Obelisk of Power; Reaver's Row; Star Spring; Emperor's Dais.

The 14 cards and 7 battlefields are the same names as the Standard list above (15 cards, 7 battlefields); the Standard list has one extra card, Fight or Flight, and 2v2 adds the Master Yi Legend ban. Per-name 2v2 effective dates are not on the hub: the 6 older cards and 5 older battlefields entered 2v2 on 2026-07-24 / 2026-09-18 (July and September articles, facts 3 and 4), and the 8 + 2 Radiance names on 2026-11-23 (fact 5). The split into "old" and "Radiance" is consistent with those articles, but only the full list above is a hub reading.

## Conflicts / uncertainties
- **2v2 scope of the Radiance wave:** resolved 2026-11-24, see fact 8. The hub shows 14 cards and 7 battlefields (the earlier 6 / 5 first read on 2026-11-23 is superseded and its table was removed).
- Dreaming Tree naming: see fact 10.
- Effective-time zone: see fact 9, unanswerable from public sources so far. Ask Riot Support or an event judge if exact-time legality ever matters.
- The regional (KR/TW) ban lists aren't published in detail. This only matters if we support those regions.
