# Tournament Results

## Summary
Riot's premier structure for 2025–26 was **Regional Qualifiers (RQs)**, 12 events of about 1,100 to 2,100
players each from Dec 2025 to Sep 2026, followed by **Regional Championships (RCs)**: Europe in Stuttgart
on 6–8 Nov 2026 and North America in Las Vegas on 11–13 Dec 2026. Next is the invitational **World Showcase**
(Shanghai, 22–23 Jan 2027). **Riot publishes full decklists itself** in "Top Decks" / "Best-Of" articles
on playriftbound.com under News → Organized Play, in a consistent text layout (Legend / Champion /
Main Deck / Battlefields / Rune Pool / Sideboard). That's the best source for our deck catalog.
Community aggregators (Liquipedia Lab, piltoverarchive, riftdecks, mobalytics, riftmana) also track events.

## Decklists captured
8 notes in `Decklists/`, the full RC Europe Top 8 copied from Riot's article. **All 8 contain cards
banned as of 2026-11-23.** Keep them as historical or reference data and flag them as illegal in the catalog.
Not captured yet: LA, Singapore and earlier RQ lists (available in Riot recaps; pre-Radiance and mostly
superseded).

## Conflicts / uncertainties
- RQ table rows marked creator are one source only. Confirm each against its Riot recap before seeding.
- Player counts marked "derived" are arithmetic from Legend share tables, not printed totals.
- "Overall Ranking" is treated as the final standing (#3/#4 = Top 4, #5–8 = Top 8). The article doesn't state the bracket.
- The ranks #4 and #6 lists (BQO Caravaggio, lets see lol) are **identical, card for card**, in Riot's article. That could be a copy error on Riot's side or a real shared list.
