# Format Catalog (H-700)

## Summary
Every play format and mode found, 2026-11-28 (extends [[Formats]]; ban data in [[Ban List]]).
**Official, sanctioned, with a Rules Hub ban list:** Standard Constructed (1v1) and **2v2 Constructed**.
Both: all produced sets legal, same deck shape (1 Legend, exactly 40 Main Deck, 12 Runes, 3 battlefields, copy
limits, sideboard <= 10 as TR 601 says for Constructed). **2v2 differs in two ways only**: (a) its own
ban list (Standard's list minus Fight or Flight, plus Master Yi, Wuju Bladesman as a banned Legend) and
(b) **teammates may not share a Champion Legend or any battlefield** (Core Rules; per-team check across two
decks). The Rules Hub lists **no other format** with a ban list. Limited (Sealed, Draft) and 2v2 Sealed are
official but have no deck-builder meaning (pool-based). Free-for-all (3 or 4 players) is an official Core Rules
mode with no ban list of its own. **No regional ban list is published** (the Hub shows one list per format).

## Other findings
- **Regional legality:** TR 601.2.b enforces regional card legality by the official product launch date. Riot's 2026-11-13 announcement says Korea and Traditional Chinese get separate lists until set parity. The Hub, read 2026-11-28, publishes **one list per format, no regional variants**, so a regional list for either format is `unverified` and cannot be built from public data. Applies equally to 2v2.
- **Future formats:** Riot says it plans "additional formats" for rotated-out sets, no details published (OP article, read 2026-11-28). Nothing else to model today.
- **Proving Grounds (OGS)** is a set and release, not a format (see [[Sets]]).
- **Not found:** any official Highlander, Singleton, Wild, Legacy, Pauper or Brawl style format. None appear on the Rules Hub or in TR 601-603.

## SPEC-002 / H-704 inputs
1. Model **format** as data: `standard` and `2v2` now (`free-for-all` only if wanted, with no ban list). Each has legal set codes, a ban list keyed by format and region, and deck rules.
2. Reuse the Constructed deck rules for 2v2 (40 / 12 / 3 battlefields / 3 copies / Chosen Champion), but add **banned Legend** to the 2v2 ban list (the Standard list has none; fact 6).
3. 2v2 is a **team check across two decks**: reject the same Legend, and any battlefield name appearing in both decks. A single 2v2 deck on its own is legal under the same single-deck rules, with a "needs a teammate deck with a different Legend and battlefields" warning. Same non-Legend cards in both decks are allowed (fact 5, community).
4. **Sideboard rule for 2v2 is unverified** (fact 4). Default to the Constructed sideboard of 10 and flag it.
5. Do not offer Sealed or Draft as deck formats (pool-based, no card list to check).
6. No regional lists exist in public data; keep `region: global` and the schema slot.
7. Ban differences from Standard: Fight or Flight (banned in Standard, legal in 2v2) and Master Yi, Wuju Bladesman (banned only in 2v2).
