# Rules Overview

## Summary
The official rules live on the **Rules Hub** (playriftbound.com; the old riftbound.leagueoflegends.com
URLs 302-redirect there). The current documents are the **Core Rules** and the **Tournament Rules**, both
marked "Last Updated 2026-10-09", with no version numbers. Patch notes and errata are published per set
(Origins through Radiance). Players score points by holding or conquering battlefields, and the first to
**8 points** wins (11 in 2v2).
